A powerful warrior prince of the Deva clan, Devaj wields twin golden blades and possesses the unique, terrifying ability to manifest his inner energy into devastating attacks. Despite his power, he faces a constant battle of political sabotage within his own kingdom. To Aadhyan's shock, Devaj bears the exact face of his brilliant apprentice, Dr. Atharv.
