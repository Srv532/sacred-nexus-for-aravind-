A brilliant scientist from our world, Dr. Sanjay unexpectedly finds his consciousness thrust into the body of 'Aadhyan'—a battle-hardened commander in a mysterious, parallel realm. Wielding a glowing blade and donning heavy silver-and-bronze armor, he must reconcile his logical, analytical mind with the ancient, instinctual rage of a seasoned warrior caught in a sprawling political conspiracy.
