Aarini’s arrogant and power-hungry son. Clad in intimidating polished black armor, he thrives on unearned accolades and cruel displays of authority. Despite lacking royal blood, his mother’s deceit has elevated him above Devaj, turning him into a terrifying, untouchable puppet for her regime.
