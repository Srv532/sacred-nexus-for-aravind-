name: "Princess Aaradhya"
role: "The Adopted Seer"
image: "/characters/aaradhya.png"
---

Radiant and striking, Aaradhya was adopted by the Queen Mother from an outer village before the monarch fell ill. Though young, her eyes hold an ancient, liquid sorrow that pierces through court deception. She speaks in riddles and seems to innately sense the displaced scientist hiding within Aadhyan's warrior form.
