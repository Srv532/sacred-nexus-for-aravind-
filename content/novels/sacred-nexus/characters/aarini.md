The Queen Mother’s niece and the de facto ruler of the Amarakarsh Empire since Devanshi's mysterious illness. Aarini leverages her manipulative charm and a network of spies to systematically dismantle Prince Devaj's power piece by piece. Her regal beauty belies eyes as cold as chips of ice.
