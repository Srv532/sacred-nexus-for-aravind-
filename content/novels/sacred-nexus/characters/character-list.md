# COMPLETE CHARACTER LIST (CHAPTERS 1-5)

| Character | Description / Role | Key Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dr. Sanjay / Aadhyan** | A brilliant scientist from our world, now inhabiting the body of a commander in another realm. | Proved existence of dark matter. After accident, found himself on a battlefield as "Aadhyan." Memories are fragmented. Can switch between scientist persona and warrior commander persona. Growing more integrated with Aadhyan's identity. Increasingly drawn into political conspiracy. Receives consciousness transfer from mysterious warrior in dream. Discovers hidden weapon that responds to his touch. |
| **Dr. Atharv** | A young, prodigious apprentice in Sanjay's lab. | Collaborated on energy field project. Sharp, intelligent, nervous, in awe of Dr. Sanjay. Has an identical counterpart in the other realm: Prince Devaj. Appears in Sanjay's dream warning about energy cascade. |
| **Assistant** | An unnamed lab assistant. | Young man with nervous energy and sharp gaze. Alerted Sanjay to Atharv's arrival. Helped pull Sanjay from prototype. |
| **Ashoka** | A warrior in the Deva clan. | Fights with glowing sword. Loyal to Prince Devaj. Given command of formation by Devaj in battle. Visibly trembles during Aarini's outburst about forbidden name. |
| **Akash** | A young warrior in the Deva clan. | Sarcastic ("old man") but loyal. Provides exposition about the empire's politics. Deeply afraid of forbidden secret—warns Aadhyan to forget the statue. Deflects Anshul's cruelty toward Aaradhya twice. Reveals Rakshasa history and Aadhyan's role in their destruction. |
| **The Rakshasa Rogues** | Hostile, non-human creatures. | Enemies of the Deva clan. Hulking, muscular, tusked, with granite-like skin and red-hot eyes. Once had kingdom Raurava, shattered a generation ago. Now used as proxy army by Emperor Durjaya. |
| **Prince Devaj** | A powerful warrior prince of the Deva clan. | Wields twin golden blades. Can manifest inner energy—a unique ability. Face identical to Dr. Atharv. Shows restraint in dealing with Anshul and Aarini. Deeply loyal to Aadhyan. In love with Tanisha. One of Queen Devanshi's "two rightful princes." |
| **Anshul** | Self-proclaimed Crown Prince of the Amarakarsh Empire. | Devaj's cousin. Arrogant and power-hungry. Commands soldiers in polished black armor. Publicly celebrated as hero of battle he didn't win. His statue is revealed to be carved over an erased figure. Called "un-blooded" by Dhrishtdev—no royal blood. Cruel to Aaradhya. |
| **Lady Aarini** | Queen Mother's advisor, now de facto ruler. | Mother of Anshul. Has seized power since Queen fell ill. Master of political manipulation. Publicly insults Aadhyan and ignores Devaj. Eyes described as "chips of ice." Accidentally says "cursed spell" about Queen Devanshi's illness, confirming foul play. Incandescent rage when forbidden name mentioned. |
| **Queen Mother Devanshi** | The rightful ruler of the Amarakarsh Empire. | Has fallen ill and confined to chambers—no one has seen or heard from her in almost a year. Statue stands in royal lineage hall. Had "two rightful princes"—Devaj and the erased one. |
| **Tanisha** | A noblewoman of high birth. | Deep blue robes with silver sigils of ancient bloodline. Intelligent and politically savvy. In love with Devaj. Worried about political situation. |
| **Aaradhya** | The adopted princess of the empire. | Radiant and striking, but with "old eyes" that see past surfaces. Speaks in enigmatic, almost prophetic phrases. Seems to sense the scientist within Aadhyan. Has unshed tears when speaking of forbidden name. Actively searches for erased prince, believing he lives. Protects outer villages. Hands tremble after Anshul's cruelty. |
| **The Forbidden One (Unknown)** | An erased historical figure. | Their statue covered to create Anshul's. Name not spoken by order of Queen Aarini. Mentioning them considered "worse than treason"—a curse. Dhrishtdev refers to "two rightful princes"—this is the second. Aarini calls them "that disgusting essence of hell." Blurry in Aadhyan's memories. |
| **The Shuddha Maanav (The Pure Humans)** | A human supremacist faction. | Believe all clans (Deva, Asura, Rakshasa) are "impure." Carry out terrorist attacks using stolen Deva technology. Their ideology is spreading in the shadows. |
| **Emperor Durjaya** | Ruthless ruler of the Asurya Empire (Asura Clan). | Rules with an iron fist. Trafficker of weapons and people. Declared eternal enmity for Amarakarsh. Shelters and funds the remaining Rakshasa as a proxy army. Waits for the empire to show weakness. Sends formal declaration accusing Amarakarsh of piracy. |
| **The Young Girl (from the poster)** | A missing child whose face appears on a weathered poster. | Symbol of the disappearances caused by Anshul's guard. Her image haunts Aadhyan's observation of the city's decay. |
| **Dhrishtdev** | Prime Minister of the Amarakarsh Empire. | Man in his prime with sharp, intelligent face and observant eyes. Bold enough to openly defy Aarini. States the Empire thrived under Queen Devanshi and her "two rightful princes." Cools his bow to Aarini deliberately. |
| **The Warrior (Vision Figure)** | A mysterious figure from Sanjay's dream. | Face hauntingly familiar to Sanjay—older, scarred, ancient sorrow. Wears ornate bronze armor with patterns pulsing with inner light. Admits failure and transfers his consciousness, skills, and memories to Sanjay via glowing finger touch. |
| **The Blurry Boy** | Recurring figure in Sanjay's memories. | Always just out of focus. Appears in dream fragments beside young Devaj. Implied to be the "second rightful prince" Dhrishtdev mentions. Aadhyan lifts both boys onto his shoulders in a memory fragment. |
| **Rakshasa Commander** | Leader of the Rakshasa forces in Chapter 2. | Surrenders to Devaj but secretly tries to draw a hidden weapon. Killed by Anshul's forces before he can fire. |
